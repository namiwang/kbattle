## Deviations from the plan

- Boundary types (`MatchConfig`, `Slot`, `Event`) are not in `types.ts`; they are defined once in v0.3 with zod.
- Vector helpers are exported as the `vec2` namespace from the package root (`vec2.add`), the `Vec2` type flat.
- Extra `units.ts` (`mpsToMetersPerTick`, `dpsToDegreesPerTick`, `secondsToTicks`): constants stay in m/s and deg/s and are converted once at load (§6.1).
- `turnToward` stops at the target instead of overshooting (the controller's example was wrong; the agent corrected it).
- `ASSUMED` labels follow the design text: cone, range, arena size and duration are marked `ASSUMED` because §6.2 marks them so.

## Carry-forward risks

- `angleOf` of a zero vector is undefined (returns 0 or 180 depending on the sign of zero); callers must guard.
