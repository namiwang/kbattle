## Deviations from the plan

- No `los.ts`: vision reuses `collision.hasLineOfSight` so vision and `face_target` share one rule (inclusive 60 m, touching blocks).
- `validateMap`/`loadMap` live in `maps.ts`; bots' helpers in `bots/shared.ts`; the stand-in loop is a test helper (`test/bots/headless.ts`) so v1.5 is not pre-built.
- Wanderer re-picks a random arena point (uniform headings hugged walls, zero shots on seed 1). Camper relocates on a hit only while holding its corner and changes corner after three decisions without headway.
- `perceive` rounds self position to 2 decimals and facing to 1 so recomputed fields match the log's Tick rows.
